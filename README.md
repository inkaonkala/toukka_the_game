# 🐛 TOUKKA

**TOUKKA** is a tiny two-player worm game made just for fun.

The characters are based on little *toukka* characters that I drew together with my toddler. I turned our drawings into pixel art and then, naturally, they needed their own game.

## 🎮 How to play

Two players share the keyboard:

- 🟠 **Orange Toukka:** WASD
- 🟢 **Green Toukka:** Arrow keys

Collect trash to grow longer.

Run into the other toukka's body to steal a piece for yourself.  
Lose all your body pieces and the other toukka wins!

## 🗑️🐛 Grow. Steal. Survive.

The game is built with plain **HTML, JavaScript and Canvas**, with our homemade toukka art.

No grand purpose. Just toukkas.
